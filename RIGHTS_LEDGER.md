## Release decision

The pilot's current public rows are factual transcriptions/derived fields from public website materials for which reuse is permitted under the policies above, subject to attribution and the stated caveats. The rights gate for **these specific pilot sources** is therefore cleared as of the audit date.

This does **not** clear future sources automatically. Every new source must receive its own rights entry before inclusion in a redistributable MODERN release.

## Remaining gates before MODERN Release 001

- independent rerun/reproduction by a person other than the founder
- reviewer sign-off on provenance and comparability notes
- release version/tag and evidence URL recorded in MODERN tracking registry

Until those gates are complete, this object remains a release candidate rather than a validated MODERN release.
