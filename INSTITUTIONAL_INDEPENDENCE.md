# Institutional Independence Scorecard

The MENA Open Data & Evidence Lab uses this scorecard to distinguish founder activity from institution-level evidence. A milestone counts only when there is public, attributable proof.

|---|---|---|---|

## What counts as external contribution

A contributor does not count because their name appears on the Lab website, in a roster, or in a private chat. A contribution counts after substantive work is reviewed and attributable through one or more of:

- merged pull request;
- accepted audit report;
- released dataset/module contribution;
- accepted methods or annotation protocol;
- validated workshop artifact;
- documented partner contribution.

## Founder-separation rule

For any institutional claim, the Lab should be able to answer:

1. Who produced the work?
2. Who reviewed it?
3. Who reproduced or validated it?
4. Who released it?
5. Who used it externally?

When the same founder is the answer to every question, the object remains founder-produced infrastructure rather than evidence of institutional independence.

## February 2027 institutional test

A credible completion state should allow the Lab homepage to explain its value without relying on the founder's biography. The founder remains Research Director, but the institution should be legible through its contributors, public releases, reviewers, users, partners, workshops, and reproducibility records.
