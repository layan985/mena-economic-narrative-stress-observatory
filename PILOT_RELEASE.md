# Observatory v0.2 pilot release status

Frozen on 10 August 2026 from commit `a33d7e20b30f4ab66b0332c02b24ffb3255b72b8`.

This is a **completed founder-produced pilot research object**, not a completed regional stress index and not an externally validated release.

## Included

- 8 release/geography rows;
- 24 populated numeric observations;
- 5 official statistical institutions;
- source URLs and retrieval dates;
- explicit period/geography descriptions;
- data dictionary, methodology, provenance, rights ledger, corrections/versioning rules;
- automated schema/count/source validation.

## Deliberately not claimed

- no composite regional stress score;
- no claim that period-average and monthly year-on-year inflation observations are directly comparable;
- no collapse of Palestine/West Bank/Jerusalem J1/Gaza series without an explicit aggregation rule;
- no external reproduction or provenance-review claim;
- no DOI-archived claim until the Zenodo record is actually published and resolves.

## Status vocabulary

The pilot itself may be described as **complete v0.2 pilot**. External validation, DOI archiving, v0.3 expansion, and outside use are separate maturity gates tracked in GitHub issues.
