# Why I dropped the first composite

My first plan was to combine price changes, market conditions, policy events, and economic narratives into one stress measure. The pilot only has the first layer, and even that layer is not internally uniform.

Jordan reports a first-half period average where other sources report monthly year-on-year inflation. Palestine's overall and regional series answer different geographic questions. Gaza's annual decline follows an extreme wartime price level, so the sign cannot be interpreted like an ordinary disinflation episode.

I considered standardizing each available column and averaging the results. I rejected that approach because countries with missing components would receive a score based on a different construct, and replacing missing components with zero would make missing evidence look reassuring.

Decision: publish the rows and the comparability problems, not a score. I will revisit the index only after defining a narrower construct and collecting matching periods.
