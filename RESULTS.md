# Results

Last updated: 13 August 2026.

## What v0.3.0-rc1 contains

The release candidate expands the frozen v0.2 pilot to 48 January–June 2026 geography-month rows. It contains 30 primary institution-month records, 18 Palestinian regional supplements, 195 populated numeric observations, and 30 unique official release URLs. Every row has a headline annual rate and a month-on-month rate.

Automated checks confirm the fixed coverage matrix and machine-readable contract. They do not independently verify the transcriptions against each source.

## What it does not show

- It does not rank countries by economic stress.
- It does not show that falling annual inflation in a conflict zone means improving welfare.
- It does not establish a comparable MENA-wide time series.
- It has not been independently reproduced or checked against the source documents.
- It is not DOI-archived and its official source bytes are not yet hash-bound.

## Abandoned first output

I did not calculate the planned composite. Although the release candidate aligns six reference months, it still mixes national source definitions, different Palestinian geographies, missing secondary layers, and wartime level/base breaks. Treating missing layers as zero or averaging the available columns would create a number with no stable interpretation.

## Status of descriptive use

The 30-row focused panel is usable for inspecting the official release trail and plotting source-defined monthly headline rates with the published caveats. It is not suitable for a league table, welfare interpretation, or causal analysis. Palestine overall and Gaza observations must retain their structural-break warnings; regional Palestinian rows remain separate.
