# Results

Last updated: 10 August 2026.

## What the pilot shows

The eight rows can be traced to named official sources, and the repository checks that the expected fields and counts are present.

## What it does not show

- It does not rank countries by economic stress.
- It does not show that falling annual inflation in a conflict zone means improving welfare.
- It does not establish a comparable MENA-wide time series.
- It has not been independently reproduced or checked against the source documents.

## Abandoned first output

I did not calculate the planned composite. The small sample mixes monthly rates, a first-half average, different Palestinian geographies, and a wartime base-effect break. Treating the missing layers as zero or averaging the available columns would create a number with no stable interpretation.
