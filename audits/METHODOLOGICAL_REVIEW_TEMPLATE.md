## Scope

Describe exactly what was reviewed: source eligibility, measurement design, comparability, annotation rules, validation logic, release methodology, or another defined component.

## Review questions

1. Are the research object and intended claims defined precisely?
2. Are source-selection and exclusion rules defensible and reproducible?
3. Are transformations and measurement choices documented sufficiently?
4. Are cross-country or cross-period comparisons valid under the stated rules?
5. Are missingness, revisions, uncertainty, and non-comparability handled appropriately?
6. Are validation checks capable of detecting important errors?
7. Are any assumptions hidden, underspecified, or vulnerable to researcher discretion?
8. What is the most serious methodological weakness remaining?
