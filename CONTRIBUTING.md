# Contributing

Contributions are evaluated on traceability and reproducibility, not title or affiliation.

## Founding Researcher Challenge

A qualifying first contribution should do all of the following:

1. identify one eligible official source;
2. add or repair at least one observation without guessing missing values;
3. provide complete provenance metadata;
4. document licensing or redistribution constraints when relevant;
5. reproduce or extend one validation check;
6. state uncertainty and comparability limitations explicitly.

## Workflow

1. Open or claim a GitHub issue.
2. Work on a branch or fork.
3. Keep raw source transcriptions separate from derived outputs.
4. Run validation and tests.
5. Open a pull request explaining the source, change, evidence, and limitations.
6. Respond to review with an auditable record of any corrections.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Do not fill nulls with zero unless zero is explicitly reported by the source.
- Do not relabel a source period to force cross-country alignment.
- Do not infer missing official observations from news coverage.
- Do not collapse conflict-affected regional series into reassuring aggregate interpretations.
- Do not claim independent verification of your own work.

Accepted substantive work is credited publicly through repository history and release acknowledgements.
