# Changelog

All notable changes to the Observatory are documented here.

## v0.3.0-rc1 — 2026-08-13

### Added

- Fixed January–June 2026 coverage across five official statistical institutions.
- 48 geography-month rows: 30 primary institution-month rows and 18 Palestinian regional supplements.
- Complete headline annual and month-on-month CPI fields for every row.
- Focused downloadable 30-row primary panel and machine-generated status summary.
- Version-specific validator, release builder, unit test, and 30-source provenance manifest.
- Structural-break enforcement for Palestine overall and Gaza; West Bank and Jerusalem J1 remain separate regional series.

### Status

Founder-produced release candidate. Automated validation passes; independent source audit, source-byte archiving/hashing, stable release, and DOI remain pending.

## v0.2 — 2026-08-07

### Added

- Pilot official-data release with 8 geography-period rows.
- 24 populated numeric observations from 5 official statistical institutions.
- Explicit source URLs, retrieval dates, measure types, and comparability notes.
- Separate Palestine overall, West Bank excluding J1, Jerusalem J1, and Gaza Strip rows where published separately.
- Conflict-related structural-break flags for affected Palestinian series.
- Explicit withholding of the composite stress measure pending required evidence layers and independent verification.

### Status

Founder-produced pilot; not yet independently audited.
