# Governance

## Purpose

The MENA Open Data & Evidence Lab maintains public research infrastructure whose credibility depends on traceability, versioning, and separation between production and verification.

## Roles

- **Director / release maintainer:** coordinates scope, release timing, documentation, and repository administration.
- **Data contributors:** collect and transcribe eligible primary-source observations.
- **Method contributors:** propose and document measurement, comparability, and annotation rules.
- **Independent verifiers:** rerun or independently audit data, code, or a defined module before a release can claim independent verification.

## Decision record

Material methodological decisions should be documented in GitHub issues or versioned documentation. Decisions that change released values require a changelog entry and a new release version.

## Separation of duties

No analyst approves their own work as independently verified. Founder-produced work may be released as such, but it must be labeled accurately until an independent reviewer completes the verification protocol.

## Credit

Contributor credit is tied to accepted, attributable public work such as merged pull requests, documented audits, annotation work, methods contributions, or co-produced outputs. Titles alone do not create scholarly credit.

## Conflicts and corrections

Potential conflicts of interest should be disclosed when they could reasonably affect source selection, interpretation, or verification. Corrections follow `CORRECTIONS.md`.
